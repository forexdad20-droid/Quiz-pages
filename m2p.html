📋 How this test works

  • 4 sections of math questions.
  • Each section gives you 15 minutes — the timer is always at the top.
  • Between sections you get a 5-minute break to rest, stretch, and get a drink.
  • While you are answering questions, there is no skipping — finish all the questions in the section, then tap the finish button to start your break.
  • On a break, you may skip the break and go straight to the next section whenever you are ready.
  • There are 38 questions in all — try to answer every one before your 15 minutes run out.
  • For 🔢 questions, work the answer out and type it in the box. For ✏️ questions, write both parts (1) and (2).
